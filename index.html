Zhaoting Sun

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, where I work with Prof. Liyan Xie and with Prof. Mingyi Hong's group, collaborating closely with Dr. Chung-Yiu Yau. Before transferring to Minnesota, I studied Computer Science at Xiamen University. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

My research focuses on two directions:

  • Generative modeling, currently diffusion language models — how decoding and sampling trajectories, together with RL post-training, determine how much reasoning ability a dLLM can actually use.
  • Optimization for deep learning, in both pretraining and fine-tuning — when and why matrix-aware optimizers such as Muon outperform Adam, and how the optimizer's geometry shapes what a model learns.
