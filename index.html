Zhaoting Sun

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, where I work with Prof. Liyan Xie and Prof. Mingyi Hong, and collaborate closely with Dr. Chung-Yiu Yau. Before transferring to Minnesota, I studied Computer Science at Xiamen University. I am applying to PhD programs and RA positions starting in 2027.

I work on generative modeling and optimization for deep learning, currently:

  • Diffusion language models: sampling on the accuracy–speed frontier, post-training for reasoning, and scaling up pretraining of full-sequence masked diffusion language models.
  • Optimization: optimizers across the stages of LLM training, and when and why matrix-aware methods like Muon beat Adam.
